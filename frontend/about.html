About SmartTrip AI

SmartTrip AI is an AI-powered travel planner that helps users generate personalized travel itineraries based on destination, budget, trip duration, and interests.

Features

  • AI-generated travel itineraries
  • Live weather updates
  • Interactive maps
  • Google Maps integration
  • Download itinerary as PDF
  • Save and manage trips
  • Suggested transport options
